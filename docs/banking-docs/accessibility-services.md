# Contoso Bank — Accessibility & Inclusive Banking

## Our Commitment

Contoso Bank is committed to providing equal access to all banking services for customers with disabilities. We continuously improve our products, services, and facilities to ensure an inclusive banking experience.

## Accessible Branch Services

### Physical Accessibility

All Contoso Bank branches meet or exceed ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) requirements:

- Wheelchair-accessible entrances with automatic doors
- Accessible ATMs at wheelchair height with tactile keypads and audio guidance
- Lowered teller counters at every branch
- Accessible parking spaces near branch entrances
- Service animals welcome in all branches
- Elevators in multi-story branches with braille floor indicators

### In-Branch Assistance

- **Sign language interpretation:** Available upon request with 48 hours advance notice. Video remote interpretation (VRI) available immediately at all branches.
- **Large-print documents:** Account agreements, statements, and disclosures available in large print (18pt font) upon request
- **Braille documents:** Account agreements and key disclosures available in braille. Allow 5-7 business days for preparation.
- **Personal assistance:** Branch staff trained to assist customers who need help completing forms, reading documents, or navigating the branch

### Quiet Banking Hours

Select branches offer quiet banking hours for customers with sensory sensitivities:

- **Schedule:** First Saturday of each month, 9:00 AM - 11:00 AM
- **Features:** Reduced lighting, no background music, limited announcements, dedicated staff available for unhurried assistance
- **Locations:** Available at 150+ flagship branches. Check contosobank.com/quiet-hours for participating branches.

## Accessible Digital Banking

### Website Accessibility

The Contoso Bank website (contosobank.com) conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards:

- Full keyboard navigation support — all features accessible without a mouse
- Screen reader compatibility (tested with JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack)
- Sufficient color contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text)
- Text resizable up to 200% without loss of content or functionality
- Alt text on all images and informative graphics
- Descriptive link text (no "click here" links)
- ARIA landmarks and headings for easy navigation
- Captions and transcripts for all video content
- No content that flashes more than 3 times per second

### Mobile App Accessibility

The Contoso Mobile App includes the following accessibility features:

- **VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android) support:** Full screen reader compatibility for all app features including account management, transfers, and bill pay
- **Dynamic text sizing:** App respects system font size settings, including extra-large text
- **High contrast mode:** Enhanced visual contrast for users with low vision
- **Reduced motion:** Respects system "reduce motion" settings to minimize animations
- **Haptic feedback:** Tactile confirmation for key actions (transfers, payments, card lock/unlock)
- **Voice commands:** Use Siri (iOS) or Google Assistant (Android) to check balances, make transfers, and find branches
- **Biometric authentication:** Fingerprint and face recognition as alternatives to typing passwords
- **Color-blind friendly design:** Information is never conveyed by color alone; icons and patterns supplement color coding

### Accessible ATMs

All Contoso ATMs include:

- Audio guidance through standard headphone jack (3.5mm)
- Tactile keypad with raised dots on the "5" key for orientation
- High-contrast screen display option
- Adjustable screen brightness
- Wheelchair-accessible height (maximum 48 inches for all controls)
- Braille on function keys and card slot
- Multiple language support (English, Spanish, French, German, Polish, and Mandarin)

Changes to communication format take effect within 1-2 billing cycles.

## Financial Literacy for All

Contoso Bank offers accessible financial education resources:

- **Video tutorials with captions and ASL:** Covering budgeting, saving, credit management, and fraud prevention
- **Easy-read guides:** Simplified versions of key banking concepts for customers with cognitive or learning disabilities
