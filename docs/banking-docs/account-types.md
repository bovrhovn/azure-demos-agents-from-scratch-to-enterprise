# Contoso Bank — Account Types

## Personal Checking Account

The Personal Checking Account is designed for everyday banking needs. It offers unlimited transactions, a free debit card, and access to over 30,000 ATMs nationwide.

- Monthly maintenance fee: €4.95 (waived with a minimum balance of €1,500 or direct deposit of €500+)
- Minimum opening deposit: €25
- Interest rate: 0.01% APY
- Overdraft protection available for €35 per occurrence
- Free online and mobile banking
- Free bill pay and person-to-person transfers

## Premium Checking Account

The Premium Checking Account is for customers who maintain higher balances and want additional benefits including priority customer service and fee waivers.

- Monthly maintenance fee: €12.95 (waived with a minimum balance of €10,000)
- Minimum opening deposit: €100
- Interest rate: 0.15% APY on balances over €5,000
- No ATM fees worldwide (up to €20 in refunds per month)
- Free cashier's checks and money orders
- Dedicated relationship manager
- Complimentary safe deposit box (small size)

## Student Checking Account

Available to students aged 17-24 enrolled in an accredited institution. No monthly fees for up to 5 years while enrolled.

- Monthly maintenance fee: €0 (while enrolled)
- Minimum opening deposit: €0
- Interest rate: 0.01% APY
- Free debit card with contactless payment
- Budgeting tools in mobile app
- One overdraft fee waiver per year
- Converts to Personal Checking after graduation or age 25

## Savings Account

The standard Savings Account helps customers build their savings with competitive interest rates and no risk.

- Monthly maintenance fee: €3.00 (waived with €300 minimum balance)
- Minimum opening deposit: €25
- Interest rate: 1.50% APY
- Up to 6 withdrawals per month (€10 fee per excess withdrawal)
- Automatic savings transfers from checking
- FDIC insured up to €250,000

## High-Yield Savings Account

For customers who want to maximize their savings returns with higher interest rates.

- Monthly maintenance fee: €0
- Minimum opening deposit: €500
- Interest rate: 4.25% APY (variable, subject to change)
- No maximum balance limit
- Online and mobile access only (no branch transactions)
- Unlimited transfers to linked Contoso checking accounts
- FDIC insured up to €250,000

## Business Checking Account

Designed for small to medium businesses with moderate transaction volumes.

- Monthly maintenance fee: €15.00 (waived with €5,000 average daily balance)
- Minimum opening deposit: €100
- Interest rate: 0.02% APY on balances over €10,000
- 500 free transactions per month (€0.25 per additional transaction)
- Free business debit card for up to 3 authorized users
- QuickBooks and accounting software integration
- Merchant services available at discounted rates
- Cash deposit allowance: €10,000 per month free (€2 per €1,000 thereafter)

## Certificate of Deposit (CD)

Fixed-term deposit accounts offering guaranteed returns at higher interest rates.

| Term | APY | Minimum Deposit | Early Withdrawal Penalty |
|------|-----|-----------------|--------------------------|
| 3 months | 3.50% | €1,000 | 30 days interest |
| 6 months | 4.00% | €1,000 | 90 days interest |
| 12 months | 4.50% | €1,000 | 180 days interest |
| 24 months | 4.75% | €2,500 | 365 days interest |
| 60 months | 5.00% | €5,000 | 365 days interest |

All CDs automatically renew at maturity unless the customer opts out within a 10-day grace period.
