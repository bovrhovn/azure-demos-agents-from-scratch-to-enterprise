# Contoso Bank — Business Banking Services

## Business Account Types

### Small Business Checking

Designed for sole proprietors, freelancers, and small businesses with moderate transaction volumes.

- Monthly maintenance fee: €15.00 (waived with €5,000 average daily balance)
- Minimum opening deposit: €100
- Free transactions: 500 per month (€0.25 per additional transaction)
- Cash deposit allowance: €10,000 per month free (€2 per €1,000 thereafter)
- Free business debit card for up to 3 authorized users
- Online and mobile banking with business dashboard
- QuickBooks, Xero, and FreshBooks integration
- Free incoming domestic wire transfers

### Business Growth Checking

For growing businesses with higher transaction volumes and more complex banking needs.

- Monthly maintenance fee: €35.00 (waived with €25,000 average daily balance)
- Free transactions: 2,000 per month (€0.15 per additional transaction)
- Cash deposit allowance: €30,000 per month free (€1.50 per €1,000 thereafter)
- Up to 10 authorized debit card users with individual spending limits
- Positive pay fraud protection included
- ACH origination for payroll and vendor payments
- Dedicated business banking relationship manager
- Same-day ACH available for an additional €5 per batch

### Business Premium Checking

Comprehensive banking for established businesses and corporations.

- Monthly maintenance fee: €75.00 (waived with €100,000 average daily balance)
- Unlimited transactions
- Unlimited cash deposits
- Up to 25 authorized users
- Zero Balance Account (ZBA) sub-accounts for departmental budgeting
- Sweep accounts for automatic investment of excess cash
- Commercial lockbox services for high-volume receivables
- Earnings credit rate: offsets service fees based on average collected balance
- Priority processing for all wire transfers and ACH payments

## Business Savings and Money Market

### Business Savings Account

- Interest rate: 1.00% APY on balances up to €100,000, 1.50% APY on balances above €100,000
- Monthly fee: €5.00 (waived with €1,000 minimum balance)
- Up to 6 transactions per month
- Automatic sweep from checking to savings available

### Business Money Market Account

- Interest rate: 2.75% APY (tiered, higher rates for larger balances)
- Monthly fee: €10.00 (waived with €10,000 minimum balance)
- Check-writing privileges (up to 3 checks per month)
- Debit card access for withdrawals
- No maximum balance limit

## Business Lines of Credit

Flexible financing for managing cash flow, purchasing inventory, or covering seasonal expenses.

- Credit limits: €10,000 to €500,000
- Interest rate: Prime + 1.00% to Prime + 5.00% (variable, based on creditworthiness and business financials)
- Draw period: revolving — borrow and repay as needed
- Interest-only payments during the draw period
- No annual fee for the first year (€150/year thereafter)
- Minimum business operating history: 2 years
- Minimum annual revenue: €100,000
- Funds available within 1 business day of draw request
