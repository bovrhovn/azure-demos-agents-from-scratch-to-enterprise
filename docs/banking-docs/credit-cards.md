# Contoso Bank — Credit Card Products

## Contoso Everyday Card

A no-frills credit card for everyday purchases with no annual fee.

- Annual fee: €0
- APR: 18.99% - 24.99% (variable, based on creditworthiness)
- Credit limit: €500 - €10,000
- Rewards: 1% cashback on all purchases
- Introductory offer: 0% APR on purchases for the first 12 months
- Foreign transaction fee: 3%
- Late payment fee: €29 (first occurrence), €39 (subsequent)
- Minimum payment: 2% of balance or €25, whichever is greater
- Grace period: 25 days from statement closing date
- Contactless payment enabled
- Free credit score monitoring through mobile app

## Contoso Rewards Plus Card

For customers who want to earn more on their everyday spending categories.

- Annual fee: €95
- APR: 16.99% - 22.99% (variable)
- Credit limit: €5,000 - €25,000
- Rewards structure:
  - 3% cashback on dining and restaurants
  - 3% cashback on grocery stores
  - 2% cashback on gas stations and public transit
  - 1% cashback on all other purchases
- Sign-up bonus: €200 cashback after spending €1,000 in the first 3 months
- Foreign transaction fee: 0%
- Annual cashback limit: €10,000 per category
- Purchase protection: 120 days on eligible items up to €500 per claim
- Extended warranty: adds 1 year to manufacturer warranties up to 3 years
- Cell phone protection: up to €600 per claim (€25 deductible) when you pay your phone bill with this card

## Contoso Travel Elite Card

Premium travel card with comprehensive travel benefits and airport lounge access.

- Annual fee: €450
- APR: 15.99% - 21.99% (variable)
- Credit limit: €10,000 - €100,000
- Rewards structure:
  - 5x points on flights booked directly with airlines
  - 5x points on hotels booked through Contoso Travel Portal
  - 3x points on dining worldwide
  - 1x point on all other purchases
- Sign-up bonus: 60,000 points after spending €4,000 in the first 3 months (worth €900 in travel)
- Points value: 1 point = €0.015 when redeemed for travel
- Foreign transaction fee: 0%
- Airport lounge access: unlimited visits to 1,300+ lounges worldwide
- TSA PreCheck / Global Entry: €100 credit every 4 years
- Travel insurance: trip cancellation up to €10,000 per trip, trip delay reimbursement up to €500
- Baggage delay insurance: up to €300 for essential purchases
- Rental car insurance: primary coverage (no need to file with personal auto insurance first)
- No blackout dates on reward redemption
- Concierge service: 24/7 personal assistance for travel bookings, dining reservations, and event tickets

## Contoso Business Card

Designed for business owners to manage company expenses and earn rewards.

- Annual fee: €0 for the first year, then €99
- APR: 17.99% - 23.99% (variable)
- Credit limit: €10,000 - €50,000
- Rewards structure:
  - 3% cashback on office supplies and internet/phone services
  - 2% cashback on gas and business travel
  - 1% cashback on all other purchases
- Sign-up bonus: €500 cashback after spending €5,000 in the first 3 months
- Employee cards: up to 10 additional cards at no extra cost with individual spending limits
- Expense management: automatic categorization and exportable reports
- QuickBooks and Xero integration
- Year-end summary for tax preparation
- Purchase protection: 90 days up to €10,000 per claim

## Contoso Secured Card

For customers building or rebuilding credit history.

- Annual fee: €39
- APR: 22.99% (variable)
- Security deposit: €200 - €2,500 (equals your credit limit)
- Rewards: 1% cashback on all purchases
- Credit limit increase: automatic review after 6 months of on-time payments
- Upgrade path: eligible for unsecured card after 12 months of responsible use (security deposit refunded)
- Free FICO score access monthly
- Reports to all three credit bureaus
- Fraud liability: €0 for unauthorized charges
