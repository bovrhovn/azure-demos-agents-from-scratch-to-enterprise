## Transaction Disputes

### Disputing a Transaction

If you see a transaction you do not recognize or believe is incorrect, follow these steps:

1. Check if the merchant name looks unfamiliar — some businesses process charges under a different name than their storefront
2. Review pending transactions — some charges are authorizations that may be adjusted or reversed by the merchant
3. Contact the merchant first — many disputes can be resolved directly with the business
4. If unresolved, file a dispute through one of these channels:
   - **Mobile App:** Transactions > select the transaction > "Dispute this charge"
   - **Online Banking:** Account Activity > select transaction > "File a Dispute"
   - **Phone:** Call +1-800-555-0100
   - **In branch:** Visit any Contoso branch with your account details

### Dispute Timeline

| Stage | Timeframe | Details |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Filing | Day 0 | Submit dispute with description and any supporting documentation |
| Acknowledgment | Within 24 hours | Receive case number and confirmation via email and SMS |
| Provisional credit | Within 3 business days | Temporary credit applied to your account for the disputed amount |
| Investigation | 10-45 business days | Contoso investigates with the merchant's bank |
| Resolution | Up to 45 days (90 days for international) | Final determination communicated via email and mail |

### Dispute Rights

Under Regulation E (for debit cards) and Regulation Z (for credit cards), you have the right to dispute unauthorized or incorrect charges:

- **Debit card disputes:** Must be reported within 60 days of the statement date. Liability is limited to €50 if reported within 2 business days of discovering the loss, €500 if reported within 60 days. Report after 60 days and you may be liable for the full amount.
- **Credit card disputes:** Must be reported within 60 days of the statement date. Maximum liability: €50 for unauthorized charges. Contoso's Zero Liability policy covers the remaining €50 for promptly reported fraud.

## Complaint Resolution Process

### Internal Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with a Contoso Bank product, service, or decision:

1. **Step 1 — Front-line resolution:** Contact us through any support channel. Our representatives are empowered to resolve most issues immediately, including fee reversals up to €100.
2. **Step 2 — Supervisor escalation:** If not resolved, ask to speak with a supervisor or team lead. Target resolution: within 3 business days.
3. **Step 3 — Customer Advocacy Office:** If still unresolved, submit a formal complaint to our Customer Advocacy Office:
   - Email: advocacy@contosobank.com
   - Mail: Contoso Bank, Customer Advocacy Office, PO Box 55100, New York, NY 10055
   - The Advocacy Office will acknowledge your complaint within 2 business days and provide a written response within 15 business days.

### External Resolution

If you are not satisfied with Contoso Bank's resolution, you may contact:

- **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB):** consumerfinance.gov/complaint
- **Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC):** helpwithmybank.gov
- **Your state's banking regulator**
- **Arbitration:** Per your account agreement, disputes may be resolved through binding arbitration. You have the right to opt out of arbitration within 60 days of account opening.
