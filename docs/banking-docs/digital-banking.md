# Contoso Bank — Digital Banking Guide

## Contoso Mobile App

The Contoso Mobile App is available for iOS (version 15.0+) and Android (version 12.0+). Download it free from the App Store or Google Play.

### Key Features

- **Account overview:** View balances, recent transactions, and pending transactions across all Contoso accounts in one dashboard
- **Mobile check deposit:** Deposit checks by taking a photo. Daily limit: €5,000. Monthly limit: €25,000. Funds available within 1 business day.
- **Contoso Pay:** Send money to anyone using their email address or phone number. Transfers between Contoso accounts are instant. External transfers take 1-3 business days.
- **Card management:** Lock/unlock cards, report lost or stolen cards, set spending limits, enable or disable contactless payments, and request replacement cards
- **Bill pay:** Schedule one-time or recurring bill payments. Set up autopay for fixed amounts or full balance payments.
- **ATM locator:** Find the nearest Contoso ATM or branch with real-time hours and wait times
- **Biometric login:** Sign in with fingerprint (Touch ID) or face recognition (Face ID) for quick and secure access
- **Spending insights:** Automatic transaction categorization with monthly spending breakdowns by category (groceries, dining, transportation, entertainment, etc.)
- **Savings goals:** Create named savings goals with target amounts and dates, and track your progress visually
- **Push notifications:** Real-time alerts for transactions, balance thresholds, bill due dates, and security events

### Mobile App Security

- 256-bit AES encryption for all data in transit and at rest
- Automatic session timeout after 5 minutes of inactivity
- No sensitive data stored on device
- Jailbreak and root detection — app will not function on compromised devices
- Remote wipe capability if device is lost or stolen
- Quarterly independent security audits

## Online Banking

Access your accounts from any web browser at online.contosobank.com.

### Features

- Full account management: view accounts, download statements (up to 7 years), and manage preferences
- Wire transfers: domestic (€25 fee, same-day) and international (€45 fee, 1-3 business days)
- External account transfers: link accounts at other banks for free ACH transfers (2-3 business days)
- Scheduled transfers: set up one-time or recurring transfers between Contoso accounts or to external accounts
- Check images: view front and back images of cleared checks for the last 18 months
- Secure messaging: send and receive messages with Contoso Bank support directly within online banking
- Tax documents: access 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, and other tax forms online
- Account alerts: configure email and SMS alerts for balances, transactions, payments, and security events

### Browser Requirements

JavaScript and cookies must be enabled. We recommend keeping your browser updated to the latest version for optimal security.

## Digital Wallet Integration

Use your Contoso debit and credit cards with popular digital wallets for contactless payments.

### Supported Wallets

- **Apple Pay:** Add your Contoso card through the Wallet app on iPhone, Apple Watch, iPad, or Mac
- **Google Pay:** Add through the Google Wallet app on Android devices and Wear OS watches
- **Samsung Pay:** Add through the Samsung Wallet app on compatible Samsung Galaxy devices
- **Garmin Pay:** Add through the Garmin Connect app for supported Garmin smartwatches

### How It Works

1. Open your digital wallet app and select "Add Card"
2. Scan your Contoso card or enter the card details manually
3. Agree to the terms and conditions
4. Verify your identity via SMS code or a call to Contoso support
5. Your card is ready to use — hold your device near any contactless terminal

Digital wallet transactions are protected by tokenization — your actual card number is never shared with merchants. Each transaction uses a unique, one-time code.

## Contoso Virtual Assistant

Our AI-powered virtual assistant is available 24/7 in the mobile app and online banking.

### What the Virtual Assistant Can Help With

- Check account balances and recent transactions
- Transfer money between accounts
- Pay bills
- Find branch and ATM locations
- Answer common questions about products, fees, and policies
- Reset your password (after identity verification)
- Schedule an appointment with a banker
- Report a lost or stolen card

### Escalation to a Human Agent

If the virtual assistant cannot resolve your question, you will be connected to a live agent:
- **Chat:** Available Mon-Fri 8am-10pm, Sat-Sun 9am-5pm
- **Phone callback:** Request a callback at a time that works for you
- **Video call:** Available for Premium and Private Wealth clients (Mon-Fri 9am-6pm)

Average wait time for live chat: under 2 minutes. Average wait time for phone callback: under 15 minutes.
