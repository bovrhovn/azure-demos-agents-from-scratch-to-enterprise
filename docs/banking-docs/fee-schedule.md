# Contoso Bank — Fee Schedule

*Effective January 1, 2026. All fees are in EUR (€). Fees are subject to change with 30 days advance notice.*

## Account Maintenance Fees

| Fee Type | Amount | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Personal Checking monthly fee | €4.95 | Waived with €1,500 balance or €500+ direct deposit |
| Premium Checking monthly fee | €12.95 | Waived with €10,000 minimum balance |
| Student Checking monthly fee | €0.00 | Free while enrolled (up to 5 years) |
| Savings Account monthly fee | €3.00 | Waived with €300 minimum balance |
| High-Yield Savings monthly fee | €0.00 | No maintenance fee |
| Business Checking monthly fee | €15.00 | Waived with €5,000 average daily balance |
| Paper statement fee | €2.00/month | Free with e-statements |
| Account inactivity fee | €10.00/month | Charged after 12 months of no activity |
| Account closing fee | €25.00 | Charged if closed within 90 days of opening |
