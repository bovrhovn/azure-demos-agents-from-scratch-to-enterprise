# Contoso Bank — Fraud Prevention FAQ

## Reporting Fraud

### How do I report a suspicious transaction on my account?
Call our 24/7 Fraud Hotline immediately at +1-800-555-FRAUD (3728). You can also report suspicious activity through the Contoso Mobile App by navigating to Account > Transactions > selecting the suspicious transaction > "Report as Fraud." We will freeze the transaction, issue a provisional credit within 24 hours, and begin an investigation. You will receive a case number via email and SMS.

### I lost my debit card. What should I do?
Lock your card immediately through the Contoso Mobile App (Cards > Lock Card) or call us at +1-800-555-0100. If you suspect the card was stolen, request a replacement card — it will arrive within 3-5 business days. For urgent needs, visit any Contoso branch for an instant-issue replacement card (€10 fee). Your account number remains the same; only the card number changes.

### I received a suspicious email or text message claiming to be from Contoso Bank. What should I do?
Do not click any links or provide personal information. Forward the email to phishing@contosobank.com or forward the text message to 7726 (SPAM). Contoso Bank will never ask for your password, full card number, or PIN via email, text, or phone. If you already clicked a link or provided information, call our Fraud Hotline immediately and change your online banking password.

### How long does a fraud investigation take?
Most fraud claims are resolved within 10 business days. For complex cases involving international transactions, the investigation may take up to 45 calendar days. During the investigation, you will have a provisional credit on your account for the disputed amount. You will be notified of the outcome via email and mail.

## Fraud Protection Features

### What is Zero Liability Protection?
Contoso Bank offers Zero Liability Protection on all debit and credit cards. You will not be held responsible for unauthorized transactions if you report the fraud promptly (within 60 days of the statement date). This covers both in-store and online transactions, including contactless payments and digital wallet transactions.

### What is transaction monitoring?
Our AI-powered fraud detection system monitors all transactions 24/7 in real time. The system analyzes spending patterns, geographic location, transaction amounts, and merchant types. If an unusual transaction is detected, we may temporarily block the transaction and send you an alert via SMS or push notification. You can confirm or deny the transaction directly from the alert.

### What are card controls?
Card controls in the Contoso Mobile App let you customize your card security settings:
- **Location-based controls:** Only allow transactions in your current geographic area
- **Merchant category controls:** Block specific merchant types (e.g., gambling, online purchases)
- **Transaction amount limits:** Set daily spending limits for different transaction types
- **International transactions:** Enable or disable international usage per trip
- **ATM withdrawals:** Set daily ATM withdrawal limits
- **Contactless payments:** Enable or disable tap-to-pay functionality

### Does Contoso Bank use two-factor authentication?
Yes. Two-factor authentication (2FA) is required for:
- Online banking login from a new device
- Transfers over €500 to external accounts
- Adding new payees or beneficiaries
- Changing account contact information (email, phone, address)
- Password resets

You can choose to receive verification codes via SMS, email, or the Contoso Authenticator feature in the mobile app. We recommend using the app-based authenticator as it is more secure than SMS.

## Online and Mobile Security

### How do I keep my online banking account secure?
- Use a strong, unique password (at least 12 characters with letters, numbers, and symbols)
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Never share your login credentials with anyone
- Log out after each session, especially on shared or public computers
- Keep your mobile app updated to the latest version
- Enable biometric login (fingerprint or face recognition) on the mobile app
- Review your account statements and transactions regularly
- Set up transaction alerts for amounts over a threshold you choose

### Is the Contoso Mobile App secure?
The Contoso Mobile App uses bank-grade 256-bit AES encryption for all data in transit and at rest. The app supports biometric authentication (fingerprint, face recognition), does not store sensitive data on the device, and automatically logs out after 5 minutes of inactivity. The app undergoes quarterly security audits by independent third-party firms.

### What should I do if I think my online banking account has been compromised?
1. Change your password immediately from a trusted device
2. Call our Fraud Hotline at +1-800-555-FRAUD (3728)
3. Review recent transactions and report any unauthorized activity
4. Check and update your contact information (email, phone)
5. Revoke any third-party app access (Settings > Connected Apps)
6. Enable additional security alerts
7. Consider requesting new account numbers if the breach is severe

## Scam Awareness

### What are the most common banking scams?
- **Phishing emails and texts:** Messages that look like they come from Contoso Bank asking you to verify your account or click a link. Always check the sender address carefully.
- **Phone scams (vishing):** Callers pretending to be bank representatives asking for your PIN, password, or account details. Contoso will never ask for this information over the phone.
- **Check fraud:** Overpayment scams where someone sends you a check for more than owed and asks you to wire the difference. The check will eventually bounce.
- **Account takeover:** Scammers use stolen personal information to access your account. Protect yourself with strong passwords and 2FA.
- **Authorized push payment (APP) fraud:** You are tricked into authorizing a payment to a fraudster posing as a legitimate business or person.

### Does Contoso Bank offer fraud education?
Yes. We offer free monthly webinars on fraud prevention and financial security. Sign up at contosobank.com/security-webinars. We also publish weekly security tips in the mobile app and send seasonal fraud alerts (e.g., holiday shopping scams, tax season scams).
