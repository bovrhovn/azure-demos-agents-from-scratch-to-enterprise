|----------------|----------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| €100,000 | from €12 | from €18 | from €25 |

| €500,000 | from €38 | from €55 | from €82 |
| €1,000,000 | from €65 | from €95 | from €145 |

*Rates shown are for a healthy non-smoker aged 30. Actual premiums depend on age, health, and lifestyle factors.*

- No medical exam required for coverage up to €250,000 (simplified underwriting)
- Coverage amounts: €50,000 to €5,000,000
- Convertible to whole life insurance within the first 10 years without a new medical exam
- Accelerated death benefit: access up to 50% of the death benefit if diagnosed with a terminal illness
- Beneficiary changes can be made at any time through online banking

### Whole Life Insurance

Permanent life insurance with guaranteed cash value growth.

- Coverage amounts: €25,000 to €2,000,000
- Fixed premiums that never increase
- Guaranteed cash value accumulation at 2.5% annual rate
- Policy loans available against cash value at 5.0% interest rate
- Paid-up additions rider available to accelerate cash value growth
- Dividends may be paid annually (not guaranteed)

## Home Insurance

### Homeowners Insurance

Protect your home, belongings, and personal liability.

- **Dwelling coverage:** Covers repair or replacement of your home's structure due to covered perils (fire, storm, theft, vandalism)
- **Personal property coverage:** Protects furniture, electronics, clothing, and other belongings (up to 70% of dwelling coverage)
- **Liability coverage:** €100,000 to €500,000 for bodily injury or property damage to others on your property
- **Additional living expenses:** Covers temporary housing and living costs if your home becomes uninhabitable (up to 20% of dwelling coverage for 12 months)
- **Deductibles:** €500, €1,000, or €2,500 (higher deductible = lower premium)
- **Bundling discount:** Save 15% when you bundle homeowners with auto insurance through Contoso

### Renters Insurance

Affordable coverage for tenants to protect personal belongings and provide liability coverage.

- Personal property coverage: €15,000 to €100,000
- Liability coverage: €100,000 to €300,000
- Monthly premiums starting from €12
- No deductible option available (at higher premium)
- Covers theft, fire, water damage (not flooding), and vandalism
- Replacement cost coverage available (pays to replace items at current market price rather than depreciated value)

## Travel Insurance

### Single Trip Coverage

- Trip cancellation: up to 100% of trip cost (€10,000 maximum)
- Trip interruption: up to 150% of trip cost for additional expenses
- Emergency medical: up to €100,000
- Emergency evacuation: up to €250,000
- Baggage loss/delay: up to €2,500 for loss, €500 for delay (6+ hours)
- Travel delay: up to €200 per day (maximum 5 days) for meals and accommodation
- Premiums: starting from €35 per trip (based on trip cost, duration, and destination)

### Annual Multi-Trip Coverage

- Covers unlimited trips throughout the year (each trip up to 30 days)
- Annual premium: €199 for individual, €349 for family
- Same coverage limits as single trip
- Ideal for frequent travellers taking 3+ trips per year
- Pre-existing medical condition waiver included if purchased within 14 days of first trip booking

## Filing a Claim

1. Log in to the Contoso Mobile App or Online Banking
2. Navigate to Insurance > My Policies > File a Claim
3. Select the policy and describe the incident
4. Upload supporting documents (photos, police reports, receipts)
5. Receive a claim number immediately via email and SMS
6. A claims adjuster will contact you within 2 business days
7. Most claims are resolved within 10-15 business days

For urgent claims (e.g., auto accident, home emergency), call the 24/7 Insurance Claims Hotline at +1-800-555-CLMS (2567).
