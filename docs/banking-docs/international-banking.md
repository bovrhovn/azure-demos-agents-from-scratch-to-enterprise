# Contoso Bank — International Banking Services

## International Wire Transfers

Send and receive money across borders through our secure wire transfer network.

### Outgoing International Wires

- Fee: €45 per transfer
- Delivery time: 1-3 business days (depending on destination country and intermediary banks)
- Currency options: over 40 currencies supported
- Cut-off time: 3:00 PM local time for same-day processing
- Maximum single transfer: €250,000 (higher limits available for Private Wealth clients)
- Required information: recipient's full name, bank name, SWIFT/BIC code, IBAN (for European transfers), and account number
- Tracking: real-time status updates via online banking and mobile app

### Incoming International Wires

- Fee: €15 per transfer
- Funds typically available within 1 business day of receipt
- Automatic currency conversion at the prevailing exchange rate at time of credit
- Notification via email and push notification when funds are received

## Foreign Currency Exchange

### Exchange Services

Contoso Bank offers competitive foreign exchange rates for over 40 currencies.

- **Online exchange:** Convert currencies between Contoso accounts at mid-market rate + 0.5% spread
- **Branch exchange:** Buy or sell foreign currency cash at select branches (1.5% spread on cash transactions)
- **Rate alerts:** Set target exchange rates and receive notifications when your desired rate is reached
- **Forward contracts:** Lock in today's rate for a future currency exchange (minimum €10,000, up to 12 months forward)

## Multi-Currency Account

Hold, send, and receive money in multiple currencies without conversion fees between your own currency wallets.

- Supported currencies: EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, PLN, CZK, SEK, NOK, DKK
- Monthly fee: €5.00 (waived for Premium and Business Checking customers)
- No conversion fee when moving between your own wallets
- Debit card linked to multi-currency account automatically pays in local currency when travelling
- Receive international payments in their original currency
- Convert between wallets at any time using the mid-market rate + 0.3% spread
- Ideal for freelancers, expats, and businesses with international clients

## International Debit Card Usage

### Using Your Contoso Debit Card Abroad

- Your Contoso debit card works at over 2 million ATMs worldwide via the Visa/Mastercard network
- Foreign transaction fee: 3% for Personal Checking, 0% for Premium Checking
- ATM withdrawal fee abroad: €5.00 per transaction (plus any fee from the ATM operator)
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit abroad: €500 (can be temporarily increased via the mobile app up to €2,000)
- Contactless payments work internationally wherever Visa/Mastercard contactless is accepted

### Travel Notifications

Before travelling internationally, set a travel notification to prevent your card from being blocked:

1. Open the Contoso Mobile App
2. Go to Cards > Travel Notification
3. Select the card(s) you will use
4. Enter your destination country(ies) and travel dates
5. Confirm — your card will work seamlessly in the specified regions

If you forget to set a travel notification and your card is blocked, call +1-800-555-0100 (available 24/7) or use the mobile app to temporarily unlock your card.

- No monthly fee for the first 2 years
