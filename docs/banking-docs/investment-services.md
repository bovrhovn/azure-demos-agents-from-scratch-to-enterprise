# Contoso Bank — Investment Services

## Overview

Contoso Bank offers a range of investment products for customers looking to grow their wealth beyond traditional savings accounts. All investment accounts include access to our online investment dashboard and mobile trading features.

**Important:** Investments are not FDIC insured, are not bank guaranteed, and may lose value. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

## Brokerage Account

A self-directed brokerage account for buying and selling stocks, bonds, ETFs, and mutual funds.

- Account minimum: €0
- Stock and ETF trades: €0 commission
- Mutual fund trades: €0 for Contoso-partnered funds, €9.95 for others
- Options trades: €0.65 per contract
- Bond trades: €1 per bond (minimum €10 per trade)
- Margin trading: available with a minimum balance of €2,000
- Margin interest rate: 8.50% - 12.50% (based on balance tier)
- Real-time quotes and market data included
- Fractional shares: invest in stocks with as little as €1
- Dividend reinvestment program (DRIP): automatic, no fees
- Tax-lot accounting: FIFO, LIFO, or specific identification

## Retirement Accounts

### Individual Retirement Account (IRA)

- Traditional IRA: contributions may be tax-deductible; earnings grow tax-deferred
- Roth IRA: contributions made with after-tax money; qualified withdrawals are tax-free
- Contribution limit: €7,000 per year (€8,000 if age 50 or older)
- Investment options: full range of stocks, bonds, ETFs, and mutual funds
- No account maintenance fees
- Required minimum distributions (RMDs) begin at age 73 for Traditional IRAs
- Roth IRA has no RMD requirement during the owner's lifetime

### Rollover IRA

Transfer your 401(k) or other employer-sponsored retirement plan to a Contoso Rollover IRA.

- No fees for the rollover process
- Maintain tax-deferred status of your savings
- Broader investment options compared to most employer plans
- Consolidate multiple retirement accounts into one
- Dedicated rollover specialist available by phone or in-branch

## Financial Planning

Contoso Bank customers have access to complimentary financial planning consultations.

- **Basic planning session** (free): 30-minute session covering budgeting, savings goals, and investment basics. Available to all customers.
- **Comprehensive financial plan** (€250, waived for Premium and Private Wealth tiers): Full analysis of income, expenses, assets, liabilities, insurance, tax strategy, and retirement projections. Delivered as a written plan within 2 weeks.
- **Ongoing advisory relationship**: Available for Private Wealth clients. Includes quarterly reviews, estate planning coordination, and tax optimization strategies.

All financial advisors at Contoso Bank are Series 7 and Series 66 licensed and act in a fiduciary capacity.
