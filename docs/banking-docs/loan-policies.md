# Contoso Bank — Loan Policies

## Personal Loan

Unsecured personal loans for debt consolidation, home improvements, major purchases, or unexpected expenses.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Minimum age: 18 years
- Minimum credit score: 640
- Minimum annual income: €25,000
- Employment history: at least 12 months with current employer (or 24 months of self-employment history)
- Debt-to-income ratio: must not exceed 40%
- Must have a Contoso Bank checking or savings account

### Loan Terms
- Loan amounts: €2,000 - €50,000
- APR: 6.99% - 19.99% (fixed, based on creditworthiness)
- Repayment terms: 12, 24, 36, 48, or 60 months
- Origination fee: 1% - 5% of loan amount (deducted from disbursement)
- Late payment fee: €35 or 5% of the payment amount, whichever is less
- No prepayment penalties
- Automatic payment discount: 0.25% APR reduction when enrolled in autopay
- Funds disbursed within 1-3 business days after approval

## Mortgage Loan

Fixed and adjustable-rate mortgages for purchasing or refinancing residential properties.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Minimum credit score: 620 (conventional), 580 (FHA)
- Minimum down payment: 20% (conventional), 3.5% (FHA), 0% (VA for eligible veterans)
- Maximum debt-to-income ratio: 43%
- Property must be primary residence, second home, or investment property
- Property appraisal required
- Proof of income: last 2 years of tax returns and W-2s, last 2 months of pay stubs

### Loan Terms

| Product | Rate | Term | Best For |
|---------|------|------|----------|
| 30-Year Fixed | 6.75% APR | 30 years | Lower monthly payments, long-term stability |
| 15-Year Fixed | 5.99% APR | 15 years | Faster payoff, less total interest |
| 5/1 ARM | 5.50% APR (initial) | 30 years | Lower initial rate, plan to move within 5 years |
| 7/1 ARM | 5.75% APR (initial) | 30 years | Lower initial rate, moderate-term ownership |
| FHA Loan | 6.25% APR | 30 years | Lower credit score, smaller down payment |
| Jumbo Loan | 7.25% APR | 30 years | Loan amounts above €726,200 |

- Closing costs: typically 2% - 5% of loan amount
- Private mortgage insurance (PMI): required if down payment is less than 20% (€50 - €200/month)
- Rate lock: available for 30, 45, or 60 days at no additional cost
- Points: each point costs 1% of loan amount and reduces rate by approximately 0.25%

## Auto Loan

Financing for new and used vehicle purchases from dealerships or private sellers.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Minimum credit score: 600
- Minimum annual income: €20,000
- Maximum vehicle age: 7 years (for used vehicles)
- Maximum mileage: 100,000 km (for used vehicles)
- Loan-to-value ratio: up to 100% for new vehicles, up to 90% for used vehicles

### Loan Terms
- New vehicle rates: 4.49% - 8.99% APR (36-72 months)
- Used vehicle rates: 5.49% - 10.99% APR (36-60 months)
- Loan amounts: €5,000 - €100,000
- No application fee
- Refinancing available after 6 months
- GAP insurance available for €299 (covers difference between loan balance and vehicle value in case of total loss)

## Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC)

Revolving credit line secured by your home equity for renovations, education, or other major expenses.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Minimum credit score: 680
- Combined loan-to-value ratio: must not exceed 85%
- Minimum home equity: €30,000
- Property must be primary residence
- Current on mortgage payments with no late payments in the last 12 months

### Terms
- Credit limit: €25,000 - €500,000
- APR: Prime rate + 0.50% to Prime rate + 3.00% (variable)
- Draw period: 10 years (interest-only payments during this period)
- Repayment period: 20 years (principal + interest payments)
- Annual fee: €75 (waived for the first year)
- Early closure fee: €500 if closed within 36 months of opening
- Interest may be tax-deductible (consult your tax advisor)

## Student Loan Refinancing

Refinance existing federal or private student loans for potentially lower rates and simplified payments.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Minimum credit score: 670
- Must have completed a degree from an accredited institution
- Minimum annual income: €35,000
- Current on all existing student loan payments
- No bankruptcies or defaults in the last 7 years

### Terms
- Refinancing amounts: €10,000 - €300,000
- Fixed rates: 4.99% - 8.99% APR
- Variable rates: 4.49% - 8.49% APR
- Repayment terms: 5, 7, 10, 15, or 20 years
- No origination fee or application fee
- Co-signer release available after 24 consecutive on-time payments
- Unemployment protection: up to 12 months of forbearance

**Important Notice:** Refinancing federal student loans into a private loan means losing access to federal benefits such as income-driven repayment plans, Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF), and federal forbearance/deferment options.
