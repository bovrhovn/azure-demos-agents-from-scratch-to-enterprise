# Contoso Bank — New Customer Onboarding Guide

## Welcome to Contoso Bank

Thank you for choosing Contoso Bank. This guide walks you through everything you need to get started with your new account.

## Step 1: Verify Your Identity

Within the first 7 days of opening your account, you must complete identity verification. Bring the following to any Contoso branch or upload through the mobile app:

**Required documents (one from each category):**

Government-issued photo ID:
- Passport
- National identity card
- Driver's license

Proof of address (dated within the last 3 months):
- Utility bill (electricity, gas, water, internet)
- Bank statement from another institution
- Rental agreement or mortgage statement
- Government correspondence (tax notice, voter registration)

Proof of income (for credit products):
- Last 3 months of pay slips
- Employment contract
- Tax return from the last fiscal year
- Business registration (for self-employed customers)

## Step 2: Set Up Online Banking

1. Visit online.contosobank.com or download the Contoso Mobile App from the App Store or Google Play
2. Click "Register for Online Banking"
3. Enter your account number and the temporary PIN provided during account opening
4. Create a username and a strong password (minimum 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols)
5. Set up two-factor authentication using the Contoso Authenticator or SMS verification
6. Configure your security questions as a backup recovery method

## Step 3: Activate Your Debit Card

Your debit card will arrive by mail within 5-7 business days. To activate it:

- **By phone:** Call +1-800-555-0100 and follow the automated prompts
- **By ATM:** Insert the card at any Contoso ATM and enter the PIN from your welcome letter
- **By app:** Go to Cards > Activate Card and follow the instructions

After activation, set your preferred PIN. For security, do not use easily guessable PINs like your birth year, sequential numbers (1234), or repeated digits (1111).

## Step 4: Set Up Direct Deposit

To receive your salary or other recurring payments directly to your Contoso account, provide your employer with:

- **Bank name:** Contoso Bank
- **Routing number:** 021000089
- **Account number:** Found on your welcome letter, in the mobile app, or on your checks
- **Account type:** Checking or Savings

Direct deposits typically begin within 1-2 pay cycles after your employer processes the change. Setting up direct deposit of €500 or more per month waives the Personal Checking monthly maintenance fee.

## Step 5: Enroll in E-Statements

Go paperless and save €2/month by enrolling in electronic statements:

1. Log in to Online Banking
2. Navigate to Account Settings > Statement Preferences
3. Select "E-Statements" for each account
4. Confirm your email address

You will receive an email notification when your monthly statement is available. Statements are stored online for 7 years.

## Step 6: Set Up Alerts and Notifications

Customize your account alerts to stay informed:

- **Balance alerts:** Get notified when your balance falls below a specified amount
- **Transaction alerts:** Receive notifications for transactions above a certain amount
- **Payment reminders:** Get reminders before bill payment due dates
- **Security alerts:** Notifications for login from new devices, password changes, and large transfers
- **Direct deposit alerts:** Get notified when your paycheck arrives

Configure alerts in the mobile app under Settings > Notifications or in Online Banking under Profile > Alert Preferences.

## Step 7: Explore Additional Services

### Bill Pay
Pay bills directly from your Contoso account. Set up one-time or recurring payments to any biller. Payments are sent electronically (1-2 business days) or by check (5-7 business days) depending on the biller.

### Person-to-Person Transfers
Send money to friends and family using their email address or phone number through Contoso Pay in the mobile app. Transfers between Contoso accounts are instant. Transfers to external banks take 1-3 business days.

### Mobile Check Deposit
Deposit checks from anywhere by taking a photo with the Contoso Mobile App. Daily limit: €5,000. Monthly limit: €25,000. Funds are typically available within 1 business day.

### Automatic Savings
Set up automatic transfers from your checking to savings account on a schedule you choose (weekly, biweekly, or monthly). Start with as little as €10 per transfer.

### Budgeting Tools
The Contoso Mobile App includes free budgeting tools that automatically categorize your spending, track trends over time, and help you set and monitor savings goals.

## Branch Locations

Contoso Bank operates over 500 branches across 25 countries. Find your nearest branch at contosobank.com/locations or in the mobile app under More > Find a Branch.

Branch hours (standard):
- Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed
- National holidays: Closed

Select flagship branches offer extended hours until 7:00 PM on weekdays and full Saturday hours until 4:00 PM.
