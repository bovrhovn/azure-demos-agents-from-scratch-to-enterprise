# Contoso Bank — Privacy & Data Security Policy

*Last updated: January 1, 2026*

## Our Commitment to Privacy

Contoso Bank is committed to protecting your personal and financial information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights regarding your information.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Identity information:** Full name, date of birth, Social Security number or national identification number, government-issued photo ID
- **Contact information:** Mailing address, email address, phone number(s)
- **Financial information:** Income, employment details, tax information, existing debts, and assets
- **Account information:** Account numbers, transaction history, payment information
- **Communications:** Messages, emails, and phone calls with our support team (calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes)

### Information We Collect Automatically

- **Device information:** Device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, mobile device identifiers
- **Usage data:** Pages visited on our website, features used in the mobile app, login times, and session duration
- **Location data:** Approximate location based on IP address; precise location only if you enable location services in the mobile app (used for ATM/branch finder and card security)
- **Transaction data:** Date, time, amount, merchant name, and location of each transaction

### Information from Third Parties

- Credit reports from credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) for credit applications
- Identity verification services
- Public records (e.g., property records for mortgage applications)
- Marketing partners (with your consent)

## How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

1. **Account management:** Opening, maintaining, and closing accounts; processing transactions; providing customer support
2. **Credit decisions:** Evaluating applications for loans, credit cards, and lines of credit
3. **Fraud prevention:** Monitoring transactions for suspicious activity, verifying identity, preventing unauthorized access
4. **Legal compliance:** Meeting regulatory requirements including anti-money laundering (AML), Know Your Customer (KYC), and tax reporting obligations
5. **Service improvement:** Analyzing usage patterns to improve our products, website, and mobile app
6. **Communications:** Sending account statements, transaction alerts, security notifications, and service updates
7. **Marketing:** With your consent, sending information about products and services that may interest you

## Information Sharing

Contoso Bank does **not** sell your personal information to third parties. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

| Recipient | Purpose | Your Control |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Service providers | Processing transactions, printing statements, IT services | Contractually required to protect your data |
| Credit bureaus | Reporting account activity for your credit file | Required by law; you can dispute inaccuracies |
| Government agencies | Complying with legal requests, tax reporting, regulatory requirements | Required by law |
| Fraud prevention networks | Sharing fraud indicators to protect the banking system | Required for account security |
| Affiliated companies | Offering related financial products (insurance, investments) | You can opt out |
| With your consent | Sharing with third-party apps you connect to your account | You control via Settings > Connected Apps |

## Data Security Measures

### Technical Safeguards

- **Encryption:** All data in transit is protected with TLS 1.3 encryption. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
- **Access controls:** Role-based access ensures employees can only access data necessary for their job function
- **Multi-factor authentication:** Required for all employee access to customer data systems
- **Network security:** Firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and 24/7 security operations center (SOC) monitoring
- **Vulnerability management:** Regular penetration testing, code reviews, and vulnerability scanning
- **Secure development:** All software follows OWASP secure coding guidelines and undergoes security review before deployment

### Operational Safeguards

- Background checks for all employees with access to customer data
- Annual security awareness training for all staff
- Incident response team with documented procedures for data breaches
- Regular third-party security audits (SOC 2 Type II certified)
- Data retention policies with automatic purging of data no longer needed

### Physical Safeguards

- Data centers are located in geographically diverse, secure facilities
- Physical access controlled by biometric authentication and security personnel
- 24/7 video surveillance and environmental controls
- Redundant power and cooling systems
- Disaster recovery sites with real-time data replication

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

- **Right to access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Right to correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Right to deletion:** Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Right to restrict processing:** Request that we limit how we use your data
- **Right to data portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to object:** Object to processing of your data for marketing purposes
- **Right to opt out of marketing:** Stop receiving promotional emails, calls, and mail

### How to Exercise Your Rights

- **Online:** Log in to Online Banking > Settings > Privacy Preferences
- **Mobile App:** Settings > Privacy > Manage My Data
- **Phone:** Call +1-800-555-0100 and ask for the Privacy Team
- **Mail:** Contoso Bank, Privacy Office, PO Box 55200, New York, NY 10055
- **Email:** privacy@contosobank.com

We will respond to your request within 30 calendar days. Identity verification is required before processing any privacy request.

## Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Account records | 7 years after account closure |
| Transaction history | 7 years |
| Loan application records | 7 years after decision |
| Customer communications | 5 years |
| Website/app usage data | 2 years |
| Security camera footage (branches) | 90 days |
| Call recordings | 3 years |

After the retention period, data is securely destroyed using industry-standard methods (cryptographic erasure for digital data, cross-cut shredding for physical documents).

## Cookie Policy

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies:

- **Strictly necessary cookies:** Required for website functionality (login, security, navigation). Cannot be disabled.
- **Performance cookies:** Help us understand how visitors use our website. Anonymized analytics data.
- **Functional cookies:** Remember your preferences (language, region, display settings).
- **Marketing cookies:** Used to deliver relevant advertisements. Require your consent.

Manage your cookie preferences at any time via the cookie banner on our website or at contosobank.com/cookie-settings.
