# Contoso Bank — Regulatory Compliance & Banking Regulations

## Overview

Contoso Bank operates under the supervision of federal and state banking regulators and is committed to full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations. This document summarizes the key regulations that affect your banking relationship.

## Deposit Insurance

### FDIC Coverage

Contoso Bank is a member of the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC). Your deposits are insured up to the maximum allowed by law:

| Account Category | Coverage Limit |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Single accounts | €250,000 per depositor |
| Joint accounts | €250,000 per co-owner |
| Retirement accounts (IRA, SEP) | €250,000 per depositor |
| Trust accounts | €250,000 per beneficiary (up to 5 beneficiaries) |
| Business accounts | €250,000 per corporation/partnership/LLC |

- Coverage is per depositor, per ownership category, per insured bank
- Checking, savings, money market, and CD accounts are all FDIC insured
- Investment products (stocks, bonds, mutual funds, annuities) are NOT FDIC insured
- To check your coverage, use the FDIC's Electronic Deposit Insurance Estimator at fdic.gov/edie

## Know Your Customer (KYC) Requirements

Federal regulations require Contoso Bank to verify the identity of every person who opens an account.

### What We Verify

- **Full legal name** as it appears on government-issued ID
- **Date of birth**
- **Address** (residential, not P.O. Box for primary address)
- **Identification number** (Social Security number for U.S. persons; passport number and country of issuance for non-U.S. persons)

### Acceptable Identification Documents

- Valid passport
- National identity card
- Driver's license (state-issued)
- Military ID
- Permanent resident card (Green Card)

For business accounts, we also require:
- Articles of incorporation or organization
- Business license or registration
- Employer Identification Number (EIN)
- Beneficial ownership information (all individuals owning 25% or more)

### Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

In certain cases, additional documentation and review may be required:
- Accounts with expected high transaction volumes
- Customers who are politically exposed persons (PEPs)
- Customers from high-risk jurisdictions as defined by FATF
- Complex business structures with multiple layers of ownership

## Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

Contoso Bank maintains a comprehensive AML program to detect and prevent money laundering and terrorist financing.

### Our AML Program Includes

- **Customer identification and verification** at account opening and throughout the relationship
- **Transaction monitoring:** Automated systems analyze transaction patterns for suspicious activity
- **Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs):** Filed with FinCEN when suspicious activity is detected. By law, we cannot inform you if a SAR has been filed.
- **Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs):** Automatically filed for cash transactions exceeding €10,000 in a single day
- **Employee training:** All staff complete annual AML training
- **Independent testing:** Our AML program is audited annually by an independent third party

### Structuring Is Illegal

Deliberately breaking up transactions to avoid the €10,000 CTR reporting threshold is a federal crime known as "structuring." This includes:
- Making multiple cash deposits just below €10,000
- Asking others to make deposits on your behalf to stay below the threshold
- Splitting a transaction across multiple branches or days to avoid reporting

Contoso Bank is required to report suspected structuring activity.

## Consumer Protection Regulations

### Regulation E — Electronic Fund Transfers

Protects consumers who use electronic banking services (debit cards, ATMs, direct deposit, online transfers):

- Right to receive periodic statements
- Right to dispute unauthorized electronic transfers
- Liability for unauthorized transfers limited to €50 if reported within 2 business days
- Error resolution procedures — bank must investigate within 10 business days (45 for new accounts)

### Regulation Z — Truth in Lending

Requires clear disclosure of credit terms and protects credit card users:

- Right to a 60-day dispute window for billing errors
- Maximum liability of €50 for unauthorized credit card charges
- 21-day minimum grace period between statement and payment due date
- Advance notice required for interest rate increases (45 days)
- Restrictions on fees and rate increases in the first year

### Regulation DD — Truth in Savings

Requires clear disclosure of deposit account terms:

- Annual Percentage Yield (APY) must be disclosed before account opening
- Fees, minimum balance requirements, and interest rates must be clearly stated
- Changes to account terms require 30 days advance notice

### Regulation CC — Funds Availability

Governs when deposited funds must be made available:

| Deposit Type | Availability |
|-------------|-------------|
| Cash deposits (in person) | Next business day |
| Direct deposit / wire transfer | Same day or next business day |
| Local checks | 2 business days |
| Non-local checks | 5 business days |
| Mobile check deposit | 1-2 business days |
| Large deposits (over €5,525) | First €5,525 by day 2; remainder by day 7 |

Contoso Bank may place an extended hold on deposits in certain circumstances (new accounts, repeated overdrafts, checks previously returned, or emergency conditions).

## Fair Lending

Contoso Bank complies with all fair lending laws:

- **Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA):** Prohibits discrimination in credit decisions based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, or receipt of public assistance
- **Fair Housing Act:** Prohibits discrimination in mortgage lending based on protected characteristics
- **Community Reinvestment Act (CRA):** Contoso Bank actively serves the credit needs of the communities where we operate, including low- and moderate-income neighborhoods

If you believe you have been discriminated against, you may file a complaint with:
- Contoso Bank Customer Advocacy Office
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)
- U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) for mortgage-related complaints

## Privacy Regulations

Contoso Bank complies with:

- **Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA):** Requires financial institutions to explain their information-sharing practices and safeguard sensitive data
- **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):** For customers in the European Economic Area — includes rights to access, correct, and delete personal data
- **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):** For California residents — includes the right to know what personal data is collected and the right to opt out of data sales (Contoso does not sell personal data)

See our [Privacy & Data Security Policy](privacy-security-policy.md) for complete details.
