# Contoso Bank — Retirement Planning

## Retirement Account Options

Contoso Bank offers several tax-advantaged retirement accounts to help you save for the future.

## Traditional IRA

A tax-deferred retirement account where contributions may be deductible from current taxable income.

- Contribution limit: €7,000 per year (€8,000 if age 50 or older)
- Tax benefit: contributions may be fully or partially tax-deductible depending on income and employer plan participation
- Earnings grow tax-deferred until withdrawal
- Withdrawals taxed as ordinary income
- Required minimum distributions (RMDs) begin at age 73
- Early withdrawal penalty: 10% plus income tax if withdrawn before age 59½ (exceptions apply for first-time home purchase up to €10,000, qualified education expenses, and certain medical expenses)
- No account maintenance fee
- Investment options: stocks, bonds, ETFs, mutual funds, and CDs

## Roth IRA

After-tax contributions with tax-free growth and tax-free qualified withdrawals.

- Contribution limit: €7,000 per year (€8,000 if age 50 or older)
- Income limits: single filers with modified AGI above €161,000 and married filing jointly above €240,000 face reduced contribution limits
- Tax benefit: qualified withdrawals (after age 59½ and account open 5+ years) are completely tax-free
- Contributions (not earnings) can be withdrawn at any time without penalty or tax
- No required minimum distributions during the owner's lifetime
- Ideal for younger savers who expect to be in a higher tax bracket in retirement
- Backdoor Roth conversion available for high-income earners (consult your tax advisor)

## Employer-Sponsored Plans

### 401(k) Rollover

If you are changing jobs, retiring, or want more control over your retirement investments, you can roll over your 401(k) to a Contoso IRA.

- No fee for the rollover process
- Direct rollover (trustee-to-trustee) avoids taxes and penalties
- Consolidate multiple 401(k) accounts from previous employers into one account
- Access to broader investment options than most employer plans
- Dedicated rollover specialist available: call +1-800-555-RETIR (73847)

### SEP IRA (for Self-Employed)

Simplified Employee Pension for self-employed individuals and small business owners.

- Contribution limit: 25% of net self-employment income, up to €69,000 per year
- Employer contributions only — employees do not contribute
- Tax-deductible contributions reduce taxable business income
- Same investment options as Traditional IRA
- Flexible contributions: contribute different amounts each year based on business performance
- Easy setup with minimal paperwork

### SIMPLE IRA (for Small Businesses)

Savings Incentive Match Plan for Employees — designed for businesses with 100 or fewer employees.

- Employee contribution limit: €16,000 per year (€19,500 if age 50 or older)
- Employer must either match employee contributions up to 3% of compensation or make a fixed 2% contribution for all eligible employees
- Lower administrative costs compared to 401(k) plans
- No annual filing requirements for the employer
- Employees are immediately 100% vested

## Retirement Income Planning

### Withdrawal Strategies

Contoso Bank financial advisors can help you build a retirement income plan. Common strategies include:

- **Systematic withdrawal plan:** Withdraw a fixed percentage (typically 4%) of your portfolio annually, adjusted for inflation
- **Bucket strategy:** Divide assets into short-term (cash, 1-2 years), medium-term (bonds, 3-7 years), and long-term (stocks, 8+ years) buckets
- **Annuity ladder:** Purchase fixed annuities over several years to create a steady income stream
- **Required minimum distribution method:** Withdraw only the IRS-required minimum to preserve assets as long as possible

### Annuity Products

Guaranteed income products for retirement security.

| Annuity Type | Minimum Investment | Guaranteed Rate | Payout Options |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Fixed Annuity (5-year) | €10,000 | 4.25% | Lump sum or monthly income |
| Fixed Annuity (10-year) | €25,000 | 4.75% | Lump sum or monthly income |
| Fixed Index Annuity | €25,000 | 0% floor, linked to S&P 500 | Monthly, quarterly, or annual |
| Immediate Annuity | €50,000 | Based on age and rate at purchase | Monthly income for life |

- Annuity contracts include a 10-day free-look period — cancel for a full refund within 10 days of receiving the contract
- Surrender charges may apply if withdrawn before the contract term ends (typically 7-10 years)
- Death benefit: remaining contract value paid to named beneficiary
