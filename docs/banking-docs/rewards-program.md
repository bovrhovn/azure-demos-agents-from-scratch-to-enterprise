# Contoso Bank — Rewards & Loyalty Program

## Contoso Rewards Program Overview

The Contoso Rewards Program lets you earn points on everyday banking activities — not just credit card spending. Points can be redeemed for cashback, travel, gift cards, merchandise, and charitable donations.

Enrollment is free and automatic for all Contoso Bank customers with an active checking or savings account.

## Earning Points

### Credit Card Spending

Points earned depend on your credit card tier:

| Card | Points per €1 Spent | Bonus Categories |
|------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Contoso Everyday Card | 1 point | None (flat rate) |
| Contoso Rewards Plus Card | 1-3 points | 3x dining & grocery, 2x gas & transit |
| Contoso Travel Elite Card | 1-5 points | 5x flights & hotels, 3x dining |
| Contoso Business Card | 1-3 points | 3x office supplies & telecom, 2x gas & travel |

### Banking Activities

Earn bonus points for using Contoso Bank products and services:

| Activity | Points Earned | Frequency |
|----------|--------------|-----------|
| Direct deposit of €500+ | 500 points | Monthly |
| Maintain €5,000+ savings balance | 250 points | Monthly |
| Pay bills through Contoso Bill Pay | 50 points per bill | Per transaction |
| Use mobile check deposit | 25 points per deposit | Per transaction |
| Refer a friend who opens an account | 5,000 points | Per referral |
| Set up automatic savings transfer | 100 points | One-time |
| Complete financial wellness quiz | 200 points | Quarterly |
| Enroll in e-statements | 500 points | One-time |
| Write a product review | 100 points | Per review (max 5/year) |

### Points Multiplier Events

Throughout the year, Contoso runs limited-time promotions offering bonus points:

- **Holiday Shopping Bonus (Nov-Dec):** 2x points on all credit card purchases
- **Tax Season Bonus (Jan-Apr):** 500 bonus points for filing taxes through our partner TaxReady
- **Summer Travel Bonus (Jun-Aug):** 3x points on hotel and airline purchases
- **Back-to-School (Aug-Sep):** 2x points on office supply and bookstore purchases

Check the Rewards section of the mobile app for current promotions.

## Reward Tiers

Your tier is determined by total points earned in the previous calendar year:

| Tier | Annual Points Required | Benefits |
|------|----------------------|----------|
| Blue | 0 - 9,999 | Standard earning rates, basic redemption options |
| Silver | 10,000 - 49,999 | 10% bonus on all points earned, priority customer service |
| Gold | 50,000 - 149,999 | 25% bonus on all points earned, fee waivers on select services, annual gift |
| Platinum | 150,000+ | 50% bonus on all points earned, concierge service, airport lounge access, all banking fees waived |

Tier status is evaluated annually on January 1 and is valid for the full calendar year. You will never be downgraded mid-year.

### Travel Partners

Transfer points to these airline and hotel loyalty programs:

**Airlines:** Contoso Air, SkyAlliance, TransAtlantic Airways, Pacific Airlines, EuroJet
**Hotels:** Contoso Hotels & Resorts, GrandStay, Metropolitan Hotels, CoastalInn, Alpine Lodges

Transfer ratios vary by partner (typically 1:1 to 1:1.5). Transfers are processed within 24-48 hours and are not reversible.

### How to Redeem

1. Log in to the Contoso Mobile App or Online Banking
2. Navigate to Rewards > Redeem Points
3. Browse redemption categories or search for a specific reward
4. Select your reward and confirm the point redemption
5. Statement credits appear within 1-2 billing cycles. Gift cards are delivered via email within 24 hours. Merchandise ships within 5-7 business days.

## Points Expiration

- Points earned through credit card spending expire 36 months after the month they were earned
- Points earned through banking activities expire 24 months after the month they were earned
- Any account activity (earning or redeeming points) resets the expiration clock for all points
- Platinum tier members' points never expire as long as they maintain Platinum status
- You will receive an email notification 60 days before any points are set to expire

## Program Terms

- The Contoso Rewards Program is subject to the full terms and conditions at contosobank.com/rewards-terms
- Contoso Bank reserves the right to modify earning rates, redemption values, and tier thresholds with 60 days advance notice
- Points have no cash value and cannot be sold or transferred to another person
- If your Contoso account is closed, unredeemed points will be forfeited after 90 days
- Fraudulent activity will result in forfeiture of all points and possible account closure
